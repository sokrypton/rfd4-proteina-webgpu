# AGENTS.md

Operational guide for coding agents working in `rfd4p` (WebGPU implementation of `RosettaCommons/RFD4-Proteina`, inspired by `../localfold`).

## Architecture & Module Layout

1. **Plain ES Modules Only**:
   - All JavaScript files must be standard ES modules loadable directly by the browser without a bundler or compiler.
   - Always include explicit `.js` file extensions in relative `import` specifiers.
2. **Directory Structure**:
   - `shared/rfd4p/`:
     - `encoding.js` & `conditions.js`: 147-token `UNIFIED_ATOM37_ENCODING` vocabulary, atom37 layouts, and condition / CATH enums.
     - `pipeline/`: Pure-JS input transform pipeline (`buildBatch` in `pipeline/index.js`) with Zstandard `.cif.zst` frame decompressor (`zstd.js`), mmCIF/PDB/annotated-CIF parser + WL entity hashes (`cif.js`), YAML/JSON condition-spec & `sel(...)` query evaluator (`spec.js`), 3D/topological stereochemistry (`chirality.js`), token bond graph & BFS path lengths (`bonds.js`), and validation transforms (`transforms.js`).
     - `features.js`: Upstream feature factories (`trunkSeqFeatures`, `trunkCondFeatures`, `pairFeatures` with static cache, `pairCondFeatures`).
     - `flow.js` & `partial.js`: Flow-matching schedules (`scheduleTsrSafe`, `noiseSchedule`, `recycleDepths`), Euler/SDE `simulationStep`, partial diffusion (`corruptBatch`, `buildPartialSchedule`), and inverse folding (`RESAMPLED_FORWARD_MARGINAL`).
     - `output.js`: Rectangular Hungarian linear sum assignment (`linearSumAssignmentMaximize`), unindexed motif insertion (`buildPredictedInsertionMapping`), internal-coordinate group grafting (`insertInternalCoordConditionedGroups`), input-frame Kabsch alignment (`alignAtomsToInputFrame`), `processSampleToAtoms`, and `toCifString` / `toPdbString`.
     - `safetensors.js` & `presets.js`: Zero-copy `parseSafetensors`, streaming + Cache API `fetchSafetensors` and `fetchShardedWeights` (keeps the int8 shard bytes; each tensor's `.data` decodes on read, so read large weights once, at GPU upload), and 10 interactive benchmark presets (`RFD4P_PRESETS`).
   - `cpu/rfd4p/`: Deterministic float32 CPU reference implementations (`trunk.js`, `decoder.js`, `encoder.js`, `reference.js`).
   - `webgpu/rfd4p/`: Recorded-command-list WebGPU engine (`engine.js` with `64x128` and `32x64` bank-conflict-free `vec4` GEMMs, `16x16` `triGate`, 64M-float triangle chunking, and FP16 weight packing), shared token blocks (`blocks.js`), `Rfd4pTrunkGpu` (`trunk.js`), `Rfd4pDecoderGpu` (`decoder.js`), `Rfd4pEncoderGpu` (`encoder.js`), and `Rfd4pGeneratorGpu` (`generate.js`).
   - `web/`: Interactive browser studio (`index.html`, `web/app.js`, `web/studio-api.js`, `web/studio/`, `web/examples/*.cif.zst`) embedding the `nvidia/rfd4-proteina-zerogpu` / `AtomWorks Studio` interface (Mol* WebGL 3D viewer, sequence dock, condition popovers, live trajectory playback, and PDB/mmCIF/ZIP export) backed 100% client-side by WebGPU.
   - `web/design-spec.js` (pure: task, chain roles, lengths and contig read from a document's conditions; tested in `test/design-spec.test.js`) and `web/design-panel.js` (start screen, Design panel, guided set-ups, simple/advanced switch). The panel lists each chain piece by piece in contig order (`designSpec(...).chains[].pieces`): `kept` (with a keep mode from `KEEP_MODES`: all, sequence, backbone, side chains, shape only; `anywhere` when unindexed; `source` when a set-up renumbered it), `designed` (in place) and `new` (a length range). Chains are grouped by where their coordinates come from (`_sourceGroups`: "PDB 1QJG", "PDB 5O45 chain A", "Nanobody (VHH) example"), one muted caption per source; clicking a caption opens the + pdb form to replace that source's chains; chains are listed in the job's order (the sequence bar's, which is the order they were added), chains made here (+ seq, + lig) uncaptioned in place. Each piece is a sub-card in its chain (chain letter on the left; the chain's × there once it has more than one item), residues (or a new piece's length) on the left and its state on the right ("A17–131 · fixed · 3 hotspots", "H99–109 · designed", "50–120 · new"), its own × to remove it; a "+" between sub-cards (not next to new residues) inserts 5–10 new residues there. Every option in the popups is fixed or free (free: the model decides; the internal values stay 'designed' and 'anywhere'), and every list ends with "free", Shape, Exposure, Interaction and Fold included; a ligand's pose is its Structure too. A residue popup (a sub-card's, or residues picked in the viewer or the sequence bar) shows, in front, its residue range (a sub-card's), Structure (fixed, rigid, free) with the Atoms it holds (all, backbone, side chains, or named: "CG, OD1"), Sequence (fixed, free) and Position in chain (fixed, free: an unindexed residue), with Structure and Sequence both free Length in Position's place, and a sub-card's Hotspots, the rest under More (`_moreFold`: Shape, Exposure, Interaction, and a sub-card's Keep only); there are no one-click buttons (they set four things at once and could not say "structure fixed, sequence free"). A sub-card's click also selects its residues in Studio while its popup is open (`_selectFromCard`, not the viewer's popup). A sub-card's Residues take one range or several ("1–40, 60–115"); once narrowed, Rest says what happens to the piece's residues outside them (removed, the default; free; unchanged, for changing part of a piece), the gaps between ranges are free, and Between gives them a length range, turning each into new residues (so a fetched chain is cut to a domain, or to two regions with a variable linker, in one popup); a Length other than the residue count turns them into new residues (`/segments/replace`), and the document remembers the residues they replaced (`segmentOrigins`, this page only; `segmentOrigin`, `restoreSegment`, `/segments/restore`; `test/segment-restore.test.js`): the new-residues popup then shows "was 7" beside Length and a Structure and Sequence, and the old count, or a fixed or rigid Structure or a fixed Sequence, brings them back in place with their coordinates and conditions, in one Undo; with partial diffusion ticked, its warning about new residues offers the same for all of them ("Back to the residues they replaced"), since partial diffusion starts from coordinates. A piece of a loaded chain has a crop bar under its Residues (`_cropBar`): the chain as it was loaded (`chainSources`, kept by a plain structure's `StudioDoc` and carried by `combine` and a chain rename; `sourceResidues`), the piece's ranges on it, the chain's other residues muted, a handle at each end; dragging in crops (Rest), dragging out brings residues back, up to the neighbouring piece and never past new residues (`restoreFromSource`, `/residues/restore`: as loaded, then given the popup's settings, those in gaps free). It only edits the Residues field, so typing a range past the piece does the same. As the ranges change (dragged or typed), the structure previews them (`_previewCut`): residues to be cut nearly vanish (`setHighlight('cut', …)`), those to be freed fade as free ones do, and only the residues kept stay selected; closing the popup clears it. Opening a sub-card's popup also brings its residues to the middle of the view, the zoom kept (`centerOn`, fork 12c2b78). Residues the model rebuilds (no atom held, not rigid) are faded in the input structure (`setHighlight('free', …, { transparency })`, fork f7a02ee), except with partial diffusion ticked, where they are its starting point. Hotspots (the chain's, from any of its pieces; a residue may name its atoms, "A56(CG,OH)", `parseResidueList`) and, under More, Keep only (the motif set-up, `/segments/scaffold`; the residues are kept in the order typed, "A38, A14, A99" reorders the motif and renumbers the chain as AtomWorks does, `_orderMotif`, each kept residue keeping its source ("A38", `annotation_src-component_1_atom`, display only: the pipeline rewrites it) so the card still names it by the entry; the kept residues take the popup's properties, so trimming an antibody chain and making it "rigid" is one popup). "Rigid" is one rigid group per chain (`/internal-coords` `group_id`, `_rigidGroup(chain)`), so an antibody's light and heavy frameworks move apart and a framework split by CDRs moves as one, and its saved targets are cleared (`/internal-coords/clear-targets`): the pipeline centres the body and turns it at random when the job is built (`materializeInternalCoords`), as AtomWorks does and the bundled examples are saved. A residue fixed "anywhere" with only some atoms held keeps only those atoms' coordinates, held atoms first in the residue (`/coordinates/clear`; a token is read from its first atom), as an enzyme benchmark's catalytic tips are given. Residues can be brought back from their source entry next to their neighbours (`/residues/insert`, `insertResiduesFrom`: one Undo, peptide and new-residue bonds rebuilt, an ordered motif's chain renumbered; `test/residue-insert.test.js`); nothing in the card uses it yet (a residue strip did, and was taken out for now). The chain letter opens the chain's popup: its name (`/chains`), its place in the job (`/chains/order`), which the model reads (chain and molecule ids, noise draws), and a fold for it (CATH, `cath_label`, `_foldField`: named folds such as ferredoxin-like 3.30.70, Rossmann-like 3.40.50, helix bundle 1.10, or any code; a new chain's is set on its new residues, which each take it; also in the new-residues popup, where a new chain is laid out). Superposition (`superimposeCoords3D`) is a rotation, never a reflection (`test/handedness.test.js`): it used to mirror whenever its eigenvectors came out left-handed, and a design with nothing held in place, aligned onto its first (noise) frame, was then shown as its mirror image, every helix left-handed, about half the time; the model's own estimate was right-handed throughout. A residue placed anywhere is read against all its atoms (`heldAtomsOf`, `allHeavyNames`), since only its held atoms keep coordinates, so its row names them ("A250 CG, OD1 fixed · position free"); a glycine joins a named-atoms or side-chain run it sits in, and an interaction is asked of each residue's side chain, or its CA when it has none. Under More, residues can also be asked a Shape (secondary structure: helix, strand, loop), an Exposure (RASA: buried, intermediate, exposed) and an Interaction their side chains (or the atoms named) should make (H-bond donor/acceptor, salt bridge, π-stacking, π-cation, hydrophobic, metal-coordinating) (`_extraFields`); new residues take Shape and Exposure. These were Advanced-editor only; pieces split where they differ and rows name them ("A20–30 designed · helix"). Residues selected in the viewer or the sequence bar open a shorter popup for the selection (`_renderPop`): Structure and Sequence ("mixed" where the selected pieces differ, left as each piece has it), Length for one free stretch, Hotspot and Include checkboxes (unticked, the selection is taken out of the job on Apply) and a ligand's Structure, with Atoms, Position, Shape, Exposure and Interaction under More (a pick shows little at first), so fixed regions are grown, shrunk, freed or cut from the structure itself (a motif by hand: pick the residues around it, Structure and Sequence free, a Length); several pieces change in one edit (`_applyPieces`), one Undo. Ligands: Structure fixed or free, swap. New residues: Length, Shape, Exposure and the chain's Fold. More folds only four rows or more; fewer are shown as they are (`_moreFold`). Popups close with the × in their title (top right; a viewer pick's also lets go of the selection), Escape or a click outside, and move by their title bar (`_movable`): once moved, every popup opens where the last was left, kept on screen, until a reload (`popSpot`); Apply sits at the bottom, always in sight. A "+" sits between any two pieces and at both ends, new residues included, so a free chain is built as blocks of different lengths and shapes (20 helix · 6 loop · 20 strand). New residues inserted inside a chain do not renumber it: with no numbering gap the placeholder takes the residue before's number plus an insertion code (`insertSegment`, "40A"), and `realizeExpandableSegments` numbers past it, so the batch and the design are numbered exactly as the old renumber-the-rest rule made them (`test/insert-numbering.test.js`). A design therefore numbers residues after an insertion further along than the input; hotspots on designs are matched through each sample's batch atoms for that reason. A plain structure (PDB ID, optionally with the chains to keep, "1J79 A C E"; a file; "+ pdb") opens with everything fixed, without buffer ions (NA, CL, on top of AtomWorks' additive list) and with its ligands' formal charges from the CCD (RCSB entries list their components' atoms without charges, `componentsNeedingCcd`); a prepared document drops the file's text (`rawCif`), so its CIF, for the viewer and for a run, is the prepared one from the first frame (`StudioDoc`, rcsb preset; `test/fetch-defaults.test.js`), so a set-up starts from what was loaded and marks what to design; Keep only takes the popup's Structure, Atoms and Position (CG, OD1 + anywhere is an enzyme site), and ticking partial diffusion with nothing designed frees every chain. "Blueprint" draws a ferredoxin-like fold as blocks (β 6 · loop 3 · α 14 · loop 3 · β 6 · loop 3 · β 6 · loop 3 · α 14 · loop 3 · β 6) from the bundled empty chain (`start`, `blocks` steps; no download); a design follows it (helix blocks Cα i→i+3 5.1 Å and right-handed, strands 10 Å). Start-screen examples are recipes (`TASKS[].recipe`): each fetches its PDB entry (5O45, 1XVA, 1QJG, 1J79, 1N8Z, 4Z18 + 3EAK, 2KL8, in label numbering; an entry's chains come from its first biological assembly that has them, `_openEntry`) and reproduces the bundled Studio example of the same name in everything the model reads (batch features compared tensor by tensor; the antibody and motif are identical; both 2KL8 examples keep residues 1–79 (all of it but the His tag), where the bundled file drops 8–27, a bug in it; the rest differ only where the bundled file's coordinates did not come from the deposited entry: the binder's rebuilt side chains, the SAM conformer, the nanobody framework's saved orientation, and 0.001 Å rounding in the enzyme) and builds the set-up with the card's own controls in a session Studio does not show (`_buildRecipe`: `buildSession`, whose edits go straight to `handleStudioApiRequest`, so the viewer is not redrawn per step; popups hidden by `body.rfd4p-building`), then opens it once, finished, with no Undo history; offline, the bundled Studio example opens instead ("Protein from scratch" always does). Source captions read "PDB 5O45 · A" (entry and its chains in the job; the entry's `_struct.title` when pointed at). Designs and live frames are put back in the input's frame (the pipeline centres the conditioned coordinates for the model and records `center_shift`; `processSampleToAtoms` adds it back after aligning), so a fetched target does not jump when a run starts. During a run the hotspot highlight follows the job's numbering (`app.liveHotspotKeys`, res_id past any new residues), since live frames are numbered that way. Otherwise hotspots are highlighted in the viewer by `auth_seq_id`, which is how the document's CIF numbers residues for Mol* (a fetched entry keeps its authors' numbering there; `res_id` is the label numbering). There are no separate guided set-ups; partial diffusion is a line in the run settings (one control: the checkbox and its noise %). The encoder treats an atom without coordinates (a side chain an entry did not resolve) as absent (`encoderSeqFeatures`), or its NaN reached every latent and partial diffusion of such an entry (6MRR) gave a design with no atoms. Hotspots are a chip on a kept piece (`parseResidueList` / `formatResidueList`). Ligands are added ("+ lig") or swapped (a ligand's Edit menu) by their CCD code: `web/ligand-ccd.js` turns the dictionary entry's ideal coordinates into a one-residue mmCIF, merged in with its pose free. "+ seq", "+ pdb" and "+ lig" add a designed chain, chains from a PDB entry or file, or a ligand; merged in as kept chains through `/combine` (a source caption replaces that source instead), so an example is edited the same way a set-up is built from scratch. The panel edits documents only through `window.__rfd4pMutate`, so Undo and Studio refreshes work as for Studio's own edits.
   - AtomWorks Studio is **not vendored**. `studio.json` pins the release; `npm run build:studio` downloads that bundle, verifies its sha256, copies it into `assets/` and composes `index.html` from Studio's own page plus the two fragments this repo owns (`web/page/head.html`, `web/page/card.html`). Never hand-edit `assets/` or `index.html` — both are build output. Live frames, overlays, sessions, edits, the selection, chain colours and live mode go through Studio's documented embedding API, `window.atomworksStudio`. To try an unreleased Studio: `npm run build:studio -- --from <path-to-bundle>`. **Right now the site runs an unreleased Studio**: `studio.json` `override` names the commit (sokrypton/atomworks-studio `live-frame-cartoon`: cartoon overlays, RosettaCommons/atomworks-studio#29, live frames drawn as cartoon plus ligands, `setHighlight`, which the panel uses to colour hotspots in the target's own cartoon, Show/Hide sequence in the viewer toolbar with no sequence-dock heading, `setSelection`, and residue picking by default with a clicked residue shown on its own: Mol*'s focus without its 5 Å surroundings, interactions or camera jump, and following the selection, so deselecting leaves no sticks behind (whole residues selected are only outlined on the cartoon; atoms picked show their residues in sticks, c17a919), and `closeDocument`, which Clear uses to empty the viewer and the sequence without a reload or a confirmation, keeping the session for Back, and `setHighlight` with a `transparency`, which fades residues instead of colouring them, and `centerOn`, which pans the view to atoms), and `build:studio` refuses a plain release rebuild until `override` is removed after a release contains it.
   - Refold (`web/refold-view.js`, the Refold row under a finished design): LocalFold's `refoldDesign` refolds the design's sequence with AlphaFold 3 (the default) or OpenBind-0 (kept chains as templates), and the refold's designed chains are overlaid as a magenta backbone trace. That code is **vendored, not written here**: `localfold.json` pins a `sokrypton/localfold` commit, and `npm run sync:localfold [<commit>]` copies the import closure of its entries into `vendor/localfold-<commit>/` (the commit in the path is the cache-busting) and rewrites `web/localfold.js`. Never edit `vendor/`; fix LocalFold and re-sync. AlphaFold 3's parameters are Google DeepMind's (non-commercial, prohibited-use policy), so the page asks once per browser before using them and offers OpenBind-0 (Apache 2.0) instead.
   - Cache-busting: `index.html` carries an import map pinning every module under `web/`, `shared/`, `webgpu/`, `cpu/` to `?v=<content hash>` (plus the `app.js` / `styles.css` tags). Run `npm run stamp` after editing any of them (`build:studio` runs it too), or browsers can mix new and cached modules for up to 10 minutes after a deploy.
   - Chain colours come from Studio's shared palette, keyed by chain id; the design panel reads it through `window.atomworksStudio.chainColor`. The palette (fork) is cool colours only, thirteen of them: amber is the hotspots' (`--rfd4p-hotspot`) and magenta the refold's, so neither is ever a chain's, and H, L and T fall on three different colours. Selection is one look everywhere: an outline in the text colour, no tint (fork 912934c: Mol*'s select and hover marking in the foreground colour, white on black and near-black on white, hover fainter; the sequence bar's selected residues outlined, keeping their colours, a run of them one box, fork c8d0b8c), and a sub-card holding selected residues outlined the same (`_markSelected`), so chain colours, hotspots (amber), free (accent) and the fades all still show under a selection.
   - `tools/`: Headless Chrome WebGPU runner (`tools/gpu-chrome.mjs`), GPU differential test suites (`tools/gpu/check-{rfd4p,trunk,decoder,encoder,generate,webapp}.js`), and PyTorch oracle verifiers (`tools/oracle/check-{batch,features,sampler,cpu-trunk,decoder,encoder,output}.mjs`).
   - `test/`: Deterministic Node unit tests (`npm test`).

## WebGPU & Numerical Invariants

1. **Differential Verification**:
   - Every WebGPU kernel and network layer in `webgpu/rfd4p/` must be verified against the PyTorch oracle dumps and `cpu/rfd4p/` via `npm run test:gpu` (`DISPLAY=:99 node tools/gpu-chrome.mjs tools/gpu/check-rfd4p.js`).
2. **No `O(L^3)` Materialization**:
   - Triangle multiplicative updates (`tri_mult_out`, `tri_mult_in`) contract over `k` inside registers in the WGSL shader and never allocate `L x L x L` buffers.
3. **Guarded 2D Workgroup Dispatches**:
   - Any folded 1D workgroup index (`wid.x + wid.y * 65535u`) must check `< totalWorkgroups` before reading or writing storage buffers.
